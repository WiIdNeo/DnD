# Souls

## How angels gather souls

As all other Demons they geather souls. 
Humans discripe them as kindly and good, as they are friendly and help the humans. Also this is for free or at low cost most of the time.
They gather souls mainly by telling the humans about the heaven they got up on mount celestial and all souls live there in peace and joy. 
So their way is quite passive by creating this kind of monotheistic cult like christians or jews around their "heaven". 
For this it does not matter there are more than just one god, as they do not really call on it but more on heaven itself. They call it to 
the most beautiful places you can be to.

And to make sure the devils do not get their victims souls they tell the lie about the devils being bad and the hell to be a firy place of pain and evil.
Therefore they refer to the possitioning of the realms having the hell in the earth far from sunlight and the mount celestial as closest place to the sun and the sky.
They nearly never do contracts about the soul of a human (or at least not this naming) as this is a big part of their anti-devil-campaign. 

> This is heavily influenced by the structure of Dante's "Inferno", "Purgatorio" and "Paradiso". Counts on the real nature of Hell are found in overview and will be made in a hell's cult-idea.

So other than Devils they only do rarely real contracts, but they do if they can claim a soul directly by that.

In surroundings of Mount Celestia you find many big cities and churches. Also on the cliffs of mount celestia you find a lot of monasteries and some monks actually try the climb, but many of them die on the way up, ready to get picked up by the celestials.
