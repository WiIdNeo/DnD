> ## INSPIRATION
>
> Even if it not really inspired by it the book-series "Chronicals of Mistle End" Shaped a lot about my understanding of a druid.

In my opinion a Druid is not a real mage. It just got the opportunity to talk to plants, animals and the earth itself. And as it can talk it can ask those it can convince and control them as long as they like it they help. 

So you can talk to 3 Types:

- Animals
- Plants
- Earth 

Also you could say you do not talk to earth but the wild spirits. Or both.

So you can level all 4 of those and from certain level of earth or wild spirits you can start getting transformed into animals on demand.

| Tier | Animals | Plants | Earth (Leben) | Naturgeister |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | Tiersprache verstehen | Pflanzenwachstum spüren/verstehen | Lebensenergie spüren (in dir/anderen) | Geister wahrnehmen |
| **1** | kleine Gefallen, Wegweisung | Wachstum lenken, Ranken als Terrain | kleine Heilung, Erschöpfung lindern | kleine Gunsterweise (Funke, Windstoß) |
| **2** | Begleiter, geliehene Sinne | ernsthafte Heilung, Dornenwälle | ernsthafte Heilung, Vitalität übertragen/entziehen | Elementarhilfe (Blitz, Nebel, Frost) |
| **3** | Rudel befehligen, tiefe Bindung | Wald beeinflussen, pflanzenhaft werden | **Transformation freischalten** (Formen aus eigener Erfahrung) | größere Manifestation (Sturm, Feuersäule) |
| **4** | Meute/Schwarm-Kontrolle | großflächige Beeinflussung | mehrere/dauerhafte/kombinierte Formen, extremere Life-Drain/Life-Gift-Effekte | Wetter/Landschaft prägen |
