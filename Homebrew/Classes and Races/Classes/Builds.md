This class system enables you to build up different build by collecting different tiers. 
Some examples:

# Pet-Ranger

So a Ranger is basically a DEX Fighter using a bow and the pet you can take from with familiar.
If you just want a pet and no magical familiar that's something you talk to your DM about and may the cost reduces by this.

# Thief

Also a thief is just a fighter using Daggers and sneaking. Stealth always gives a bonus on damage so that's fine.

## Assassin

If you want to use poison as well go for the potion trait of the witch.

## Stalker

If you want to sneak and stealth use some moon magic

> Of course for this system the actual cost are important. This will be worked out another day.

# Monk

A monk is a DEX-Fighter using the unarmed Attack Feat and the Druid Life Traits. For flavor you may reduce the weight of Adrenalin or buff the calamity bonus action.

# Clerics and Paladins

Those are basically Fighters which connect with a god or a principle as warlock. Therefor it is just a passive Warlock.
