Other than celestials devils are cooperative to humans if they want, but they do not need to be.
They often sign contracts to help humans in life and therefore get their soul after their death. But they do not always want the soul of a human. Depending on the value the assign the service/things they  help the humans with they also want ressources or gold.

Each devil does build up their own storage of gold and ressources to be able to fullfill contracts easily.

But devils also cooperate. They are organised in hirachy based on power (connected to their soul storage). But devils respect each other no matter what rank they got and thievery of souls is rarely, also because that devil may get killed by others to save their own storage.
